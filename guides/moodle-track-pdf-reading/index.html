How to see who has read a PDF in Moodle

Moodle’s “Activity completion” report shows whether an activity is done. For a required document you usually want to know a little more: who has read the required pages, and when did they confirm? Leafr’s Overview shows exactly this for each participant and saves it as a CSV file.

Updated 5 November 2026

What you need

  • A Leafr activity, ideally with required pages and a read confirmation (see How to require reading and a read confirmation)
  • A teacher role in the course (capability “View the Leafr reading report”)

Step by step

Step 1 of 3
  1. Step 1 of 3

    Open the overview

    Open the Leafr activity and click Overview in the activity menu.

  2. Step 2 of 3

    Read the table

    One row per participant: Completed, Required pages read (or the percentage read, depending on the rule) and Confirmed on with date and time. If the course uses groups, a group filter appears; with separate groups, teachers only see their own groups.

  3. Step 3 of 3

    Export as CSV

    Download as CSV saves the same table, for example as evidence of a safety briefing. The file opens in Excel, LibreOffice or Numbers.

Good to know

What the overview leaves out

No reading times, no individual page views, no bookmarks or notes. That is deliberate: the overview shows only what is needed as evidence.

Moodle’s own log

Moodle’s standard log still records when a person first opened each page, as it does for other activities, and deletes these entries according to the site’s log retention settings.

Other reports

Leafr also appears in Moodle’s “Activity completion” report and in course completion.
