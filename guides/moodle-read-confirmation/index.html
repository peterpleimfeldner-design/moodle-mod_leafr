How to require reading and a read confirmation in Moodle

For safety briefings, policies or onboarding handbooks, “the file was opened” is weak evidence. Moodle’s standard completion for a file records no more than that. With Leafr you mark the chapters that must be read and add a statement that students confirm afterwards. Moodle marks the activity as done only when both are met.

Updated 5 November 2026

What you need

  • A Leafr activity with the PDF (see How to show a PDF as a flipbook)
  • “Enable completion tracking” switched on in the course settings
  • Chapters saved in the activity, if you want to select chapters instead of typing page numbers

Step by step

Step 1 of 6
  1. Step 1 of 6

    Choose the completion rule

    Open the activity settings and go to Completion conditions. Choose Add requirements, tick Students must view pages of the document and set Pages to view to Specific pages or chapters must be viewed.

  2. Step 2 of 6

    Select pages or chapters

    Type the pages into Required pages, for example “5-6, 9-10, 13-14”, or select whole chapters in the list below (Ctrl or Cmd for several). Leafr adds the pages of the selected chapters when you save.

  3. Step 3 of 6

    Write the confirmation text

    Tick Require read confirmation and enter the statement students confirm, for example: “I have read the required chapters and will follow the safety rules.” Then save.

  4. Step 4 of 6

    Students see what is required

    In the contents, required chapters are marked Required. Below the book a counter shows how many required pages have been read, for example “2 of 6 required pages read”.

  5. Step 5 of 6

    Confirm after reading

    Once all required pages have been viewed, the confirmation appears below the book. Students tick I confirm the statement above and click Confirm. Leafr stores the date and time.

  6. Step 6 of 6

    The activity is done

    On the course page the activity now shows Done. Restrictions, badges and course completion can use it like any other activity.

Good to know

What “viewed” means

A page counts as viewed when it was shown on screen. Leafr deliberately does not measure reading time. The confirmation is the student’s own statement that they have read the document.

No early confirmation

Before all required pages have been viewed, Leafr does not accept a confirmation, also not through a manipulated request.

Changing the rule later

Once students have completion data, Moodle locks the completion settings. To change them, click Unlock completion settings. When you save, Moodle deletes the completion data and recalculates it where possible.
