Leafr guides: PDFs in Moodle, step by step

Each guide shows one task in Moodle with screenshots from a real course. The screenshots use Moodle 5.0 with the Boost theme; other themes look slightly different.

  • How to show a PDF as a flipbook in MoodleAdd a PDF to a course so it opens as a book with contents, search and bookmarks.
  • How to require reading and a read confirmation in MoodleMark required chapters and ask for a confirmation; the activity is only done when both are met.
  • How to see who has read a PDF in MoodleOne table per activity: who has read the required pages and when they confirmed. Exports to CSV.
